Studio Loom

Studio Loom is a contemporary Indian interior design studio based in Gurugram, Haryana. The studio designs residential, commercial, and hospitality interiors across Delhi NCR. Studio Loom offers three services: turnkey interior design, design consultation, and interior styling. The studio is grounded in the principle that every space should be constructed, not assembled, built from spatial logic and material consideration. Studio Loom works with precision, material intelligence, and an unhurried process, with residential clients across Gurugram and Delhi NCR, and with commercial and hospitality clients across India.

Philosophy

Constructed,
not assembled.

Every Studio Loom project begins with how the space is used. Spatial planning precedes material selection. Material selection precedes furniture specification. Every decision is held against one question: does this serve the way you actually live?

Nothing is placed without understanding its relationship to the room.

This is what we mean by constructed.

The result is a home that works precisely and feels specific — to you, to your way of living, to the material depth that Indian craft brings to a contemporary space.

Structure Meets Story

The logic of a loom.

The name Studio Loom comes from the logic of a loom. A loom brings threads into order, warp and weft working together to produce something coherent.

Warp is structure: spatial planning, layout, proportion, the architecture of the room. Weft is story: materials, Indian craft vocabulary, texture, the layering of detail that gives a space its character.

India has one of the world's deepest craft traditions, in handloom, inlay, metalwork, and material making. Studio Loom does not replicate this heritage. It translates it. Handloom grids become screen partitions. Inlay craft becomes material geometry. The reference is Indian. The result is contemporary.
