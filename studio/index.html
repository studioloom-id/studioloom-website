Studio Loom

Good spaces are built,
not filled.

Studio Loom is a contemporary Indian interior design studio based in Gurugram. We work across residential, commercial, and hospitality spaces, with a focus on creating interiors that feel considered, connected, and made for how they are used.

Philosophy

Constructed,
not assembled.

Every Studio Loom project begins with how the space is used. Spatial planning precedes material selection. Material selection precedes furniture specification. Every decision is held against one question: does this serve the way you actually live?

Nothing is placed without understanding its relationship to the room.

This is what we mean by constructed.

The result is a home that works precisely and feels specific to you, to your way of living, to the material depth that Indian craft brings to a contemporary space.

Structure Meets Story

The logic of a loom.

Studio Loom takes its name from the idea of individual threads coming together to make something whole. We see interiors in much the same way: structure creates the foundation, while materials, craft, texture, and detail give a space its character.

India's rich craft traditions are an important reference point for our work. We reinterpret them rather than recreate them, bringing familiar patterns, techniques, and materials into contemporary spaces.

The reference is Indian. The expression is contemporary.
