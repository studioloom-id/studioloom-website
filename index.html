Our Belief

Every space is constructed,
not assembled.

Studio Loom designs at the intersection of modern structure and Indian craft sensibility. Every space is constructed, not assembled — shaped through an unhurried process of spatial planning, precision, and layering rather than a copy-paste approach.

Architecture, furniture, lighting, materials, and user behaviour are considered as one interconnected system. We use rhythm, repetition, and material continuity to create spaces that are calm, understated, intelligent, and made to last.

Our Process

How every project is built.
