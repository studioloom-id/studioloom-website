Privacy Policy

Studio Loom ("we," "us," "our") operates studioloom.co.in. This page explains what information we collect from visitors and enquirers, how we use it, and how to contact us about it. Last updated: 2026.

Information we collect

When you submit the enquiry form on our Contact page, we collect the information you provide directly: name, phone number, email address, project type, location, and any message you send. We do not require this information to browse the site.

How we use it

We use enquiry information solely to respond to your project enquiry and to communicate with you about a potential or active project. We do not sell or rent your information to third parties.

Analytics

We use Google Analytics to understand how visitors use this site (pages viewed, general location, device type). This data is aggregated and does not identify you personally. You can opt out of Google Analytics tracking using your browser's privacy settings or available opt-out extensions.

Third-party services

Our enquiry form is processed by a third-party form service that delivers submissions to our email. Our booking link uses Calendly. These providers process your data under their own privacy policies.

Your rights

You may request that we delete any information you've submitted to us, or ask what information we hold about you, by emailing us at the address below.
