The Record Bar
Gurugram

Hospitality · Gurugram · Full-Scope

A hospitality interior design project by Studio Loom: a moody record bar and lounge built around vinyl, velvet, and warm low light. Fluted wood paneling and backlit shelving frame the bar, with a working turntable and record wall.

Low, deep-set seating clusters, travertine side tables, and a patterned rug ground the lounge against the dark palette.
