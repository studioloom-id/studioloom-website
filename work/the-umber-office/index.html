The Umber Office
Noida

Commercial · Noida · Full-Scope

Walk in, and this Noida office doesn't announce itself the way commercial spaces usually do. The reception reads more like a considered entryway than a waiting room, dark veined marble and warm wood instead of glass and chrome.

Studio Loom's brief for this interior design project was less about square footage than tone: a workplace that feels unhurried, where the boardroom and the breakout bar carry the same restraint as the private cabins. Nothing here is shouting for attention, and that's deliberate.
