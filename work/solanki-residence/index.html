Solanki Residence
Gurugram

Residential · Gurugram · Full-Scope

This Gurugram home came together room by room: an entry foyer in travertine and brass, a family room built around a terracotta sofa, a dining space anchored by a walnut table, and a run of bedrooms each given their own character. The brief was a family home that felt layered and lived-in rather than showroom-perfect, so the layout and material palette were settled before a single piece of furniture was chosen.

Terracotta, cane, and walnut carry through the living and dining areas, with brass detailing, the foyer chandelier, dining pendants, mirror frames, repeated just often enough to tie the rooms together without feeling matched. It's a warmer, more textured take on contemporary Indian interiors, built to be lived in rather than looked at.
