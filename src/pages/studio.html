Studio Loom

Good spaces are built,
not filled.

Studio Loom is a contemporary Indian interior design studio based in Gurugram. We work across residential, commercial, and hospitality spaces, with a focus on creating interiors that feel considered, connected, and made for how they are used.

Our approach begins with the space itself — its structure, proportions, light, movement, and materiality. Furniture, lighting, finishes, and details follow from this thinking. We take an unhurried approach to design, giving each decision the time and attention it needs.

Philosophy

Constructed,
not assembled.

Every Studio Loom project begins with how the space is used. Spatial planning precedes material selection. Material selection precedes furniture specification. Every decision is held against one question: does this serve the way you actually live?

Nothing is placed without understanding its relationship to the room.

This is what we mean by constructed.

The result is a home that works precisely and feels specific — to you, to your way of living, to the material depth that Indian craft brings to a contemporary space.

Structure Meets Story

The logic of a loom.

Studio Loom takes its name from the simple idea behind a loom: individual threads come together to make something whole. We see interiors in much the same way. The structure of a space sets the foundation — its layout, proportion, movement, and light. The materials, craft, textures, and details bring character to it.

India has a rich tradition of making, from handloom and inlay to metalwork and stone. We look to these traditions for inspiration, without trying to recreate them. A familiar pattern, technique, or material can be reinterpreted in a new way — a handloom grid becoming a screen, or an inlay technique becoming part of a wall or floor.

The reference may be Indian. The expression is contemporary.
