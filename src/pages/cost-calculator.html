The Factors

What Affects Interior Design Cost in Gurugram?

Interior design project costs in Gurugram vary based on five factors. Understanding them gives a more accurate picture than any square-foot rate.

Property size

A 2BHK and a 3BHK involve different volumes of spatial planning, material specification, and execution. Larger apartments have higher project investment across all service types.

Scope of work

A full-home project is priced differently from a specific-room project. Scope is defined at brief stage before any work begins.

Finish level

Premium, luxury, and ultra-luxury finishes involve different material categories, fabrication requirements, and specification depth. Finish level has the largest single impact on project cost after scope.

Degree of customisation

Custom furniture, custom joinery, and bespoke material applications require more design and execution time than sourced-and-specified alternatives.

Site conditions

Access conditions, building restrictions, existing fit-out, and any civil requirements affect execution complexity and cost. These are assessed during the initial consultation.
