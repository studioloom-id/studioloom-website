Positioning

A well-considered interior works better before it looks better. It moves with you through the day, holds what matters in the right place, and carries its material depth into years of use.

Studio Loom designs from one principle: every space should be constructed, not assembled. Spatial planning before furniture selection. Materials chosen for how they age. Design decisions made in service of the people who live in the space.
