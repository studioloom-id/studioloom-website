Solanki Residence
Gurugram

Residential · Gurugram · Full-Scope

A Gurugram family home built room by room: an entry foyer in travertine and brass, a family room around a terracotta sofa, a dining space anchored by a walnut table, and bedrooms with their own character. The brief was layered and lived-in rather than showroom-perfect, so layout and palette were settled before any furniture.

Terracotta, cane, and walnut carry through the living and dining areas, tied together by repeated brass detailing: a warmer, more textured take on contemporary Indian interiors.
