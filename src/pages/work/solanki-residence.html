Solanki Residence

GurugramResidential · Full-Scope

Make it layered and lived-in. A family home that never feels like a showroom, that was the brief.

The home was designed room by room: an entry foyer in travertine and brass, a family room built around a terracotta sofa, a dining space anchored by a walnut table, and bedrooms that each keep their own character.

We settled the layout and material palette before a single piece of furniture was chosen. Terracotta, cane, and walnut carry through the living and dining areas, tied together by repeated brass detailing. The result feels warm, textured, and made for the way this family lives.
