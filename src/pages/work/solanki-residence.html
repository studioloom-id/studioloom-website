Solanki Residence
Gurugram

Residential · Gurugram · Full-Scope

A residential interior design project designed and delivered by Studio Loom in Gurugram: living, dining, bedrooms, and bathroom, resolved as one considered whole. Spatial planning came first: layout, proportion, and material continuity across every room, before any furniture was chosen.

The material palette carries warm sand tones, terracotta, cane, and brass detailing throughout, contemporary in form, grounded in Indian craft and material sensibility.
