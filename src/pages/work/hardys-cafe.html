Hardy's Cafe
Gurugram

Hospitality · Gurugram · Full-Scope

A hospitality interior design project designed and delivered by Studio Loom: a candlelit courtyard restaurant set within a heritage haveli, resolved as one considered sequence of arrival, archway, and table. Spatial planning came first: the walk from entry to seat, before any furniture was chosen.

The material palette carries carved stone, bamboo, and warm lantern light throughout, contemporary in service, grounded in the architecture of the original haveli.
