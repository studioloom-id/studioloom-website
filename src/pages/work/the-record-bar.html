The Record Bar,
Gurugram

Hospitality · Gurugram · Full-Scope

A hospitality interior design project designed and delivered by Studio Loom: a moody record bar and lounge built around vinyl, velvet, and warm low light. Fluted wood paneling and backlit shelving frame the bar, while a working turntable and record wall set the tone for the room.

Seating is arranged in low, deep-set clusters, travertine side tables and a patterned rug grounding the lounge against the dark palette carried through the rest of the space.
