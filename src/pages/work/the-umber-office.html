The Umber Office
Noida

Commercial · Noida · Full-Scope

This Noida office doesn't announce itself the way commercial spaces usually do. Reception reads like a considered entryway, with dark veined marble and warm wood instead of glass and chrome.

The brief was tone over square footage: an unhurried workplace where the boardroom and breakout bar carry the same restraint as the private cabins.
