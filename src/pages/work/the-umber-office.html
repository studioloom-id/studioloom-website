The Umber Office,
Gurugram

Commercial · Gurugram · Full-Scope

A commercial interior design project designed and delivered by Studio Loom in Gurugram: reception, private cabins, open workstations, a breakout bar, and a boardroom, resolved as one considered workplace. Spatial planning came first: layout, flow, and material continuity across every zone, before any furniture was chosen.

The material palette carries warm walnut, travertine, and brass detailing throughout, contemporary in form, grounded in a quiet, considered material sensibility.
