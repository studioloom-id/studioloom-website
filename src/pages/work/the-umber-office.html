The Umber Office
Noida

Commercial · Noida · Full-Scope

This office in Noida was planned zone by zone: a reception built around a travertine console and sculptural pendant lighting, private cabins finished in walnut with backlit shelving, and open workstations that lead into a breakout bar and boardroom. The brief was a workplace that felt considered rather than corporate, so circulation and material choices were worked out before any furniture was specified.

Walnut, travertine, and brass carry through every zone, cane-back chairs in the boardroom, woven barstools at the breakout bar, without the palette repeating itself room to room. It's a quieter, more tactile take on a commercial interior: still built for a working day, just not at the cost of warmth.
