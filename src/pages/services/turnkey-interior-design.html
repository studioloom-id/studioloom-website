Turnkey Interior Design,
Gurugram and Delhi NCR

When you commission turnkey interior design at Studio Loom, you receive a finished home. Designed and executed under one scope, with a single point of accountability from the first spatial plan to the final styled handover. You do not coordinate between separate teams. You do not manage the process. You move into a home that reflects your brief.

Turnkey interior design means one team manages the entire project: design, material procurement, site execution, and styling. Studio Loom is a design studio that also manages execution. The space you receive is the space that was designed.

Studio Loom delivers turnkey interior design across residential, commercial, and hospitality projects in Gurugram and across Delhi NCR.

Who It's For
  • Clients purchasing a new property who want a finished home. Studio Loom manages every detail so you do not have to.
  • Clients relocating from another city or country
  • Businesses commissioning a commercial or hospitality space requiring single-point management
What's Included
  • Paid consultation and project brief
  • Spatial planning and design development
  • Material and finish selection and specification
  • Custom furniture design and coordination
  • Procurement management
  • Site supervision and execution management
  • Styling and final handover
How It Begins

Studio Loom works with a select number of projects at any given time, ensuring each project receives the full attention of the studio. Every turnkey engagement begins with a structured design consultation, a dedicated session to understand your space, brief, and project requirements. The consultation fee is credited toward your project investment when you move forward.
