Interior Design Consultation,
Gurugram and Delhi NCR

Studio Loom's design consultation service gives you a complete design package built for your specific space. Spatial plans, material and finish specifications, furniture schedules, lighting design, and detailed drawings — precise enough for your contractor to execute without ambiguity.

The service can be brought in at any stage of a project, from a blank space to one that is partially built or mid-renovation. What you receive is a resolved design: drawings and specifications detailed enough for your contractor to execute with full clarity.

Who It's For
  • Clients who have an existing contractor or prefer to manage execution independently
  • Projects where design direction and specification are required without full project management
  • Clients who want Studio Loom's design thinking applied to a project they will oversee
What's Included
  • Paid consultation and project brief
  • Spatial planning and layout
  • Design concept and direction
  • Material, finish, and colour specification
  • Furniture schedule and specification
  • Lighting design
  • Detailed drawings and documentation for contractor use
How It Begins

We begin with a structured design consultation, a dedicated session to understand your space, brief, and project requirements. The consultation fee is credited toward your project investment when you move forward.
