Studio Loom Consult
Gurugram, Delhi NCR & Pan India

A focused first visit to your space, before you commit to a project. We walk through it with you, understand how it will be used, and leave you with clear direction on layout, budget and next steps.

Book on WhatsApp Send a brief

Who it's for

  • A new home, just handed over
  • A home you plan to renovate
  • An office or clinic you're leasing or fitting out
  • A café, restaurant or boutique stay in the making

What happens on site

  1. We walk through every space with you and understand how it will be lived in or worked in
  2. We review what shapes the interiors: services, ceilings, openings, light and layout
  3. We note what works, what doesn't, and what is cheaper to resolve now than later
  4. We talk through budget and timeline, honestly

What you receive, within [48 hours]

  • A short summary of observations and priorities
  • Your plan, marked up with layout direction
  • A budget range and a realistic timeline
  • A recommended next step, if you'd like to continue with us

Fee

Homes up to [size]: ₹[amount]. Larger homes, offices, clinics and hospitality spaces: quoted by size.

Good to know

The Consult is a design-level review. It is not a structural, legal or engineering inspection, and it does not include construction drawings; those are part of our Design Consultation and Turnkey services.
