Hospitality Interior Design,
India

Guests experience a hospitality space before they experience the product. How they enter, where light falls, how materials read at that hour, how the room holds its quality across a full service — these things register before the menu is opened or the room key is handed over. Studio Loom designs restaurants, bars, cafes, and boutique hotel spaces with that sequence in mind.

What We Design
  • Restaurants and dining spaces
  • Bars and beverage concepts
  • Cafes and all-day dining environments
  • Boutique hotels and guesthouses
Our Approach to Hospitality

Hospitality interiors are designed around sequence and experience. Studio Loom considers how a guest moves through the space, where the eye rests, how materials read under task and ambient lighting, and how the environment holds its character across a full service. The same spatial discipline that defines our residential work applies here.

How We Work

Studio Loom works across three service types for hospitality projects:

  • Turnkey Interior Design — Full project management from concept through to opening.
  • Design Consultation — Complete design documentation for client-managed execution.
  • Interior Styling — Considered finishing and curation.
