After styling, a space feels resolved. Every piece serves the room and the room as a whole. Studio Loom selects furniture, textiles, and objects in proportion to the room, working from what the space needs, how it reads in its specific light, and how each material relationship holds together.

The service is suited to homes that are newly constructed or ready for a considered refresh.
