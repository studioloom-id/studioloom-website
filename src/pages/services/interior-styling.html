Interior Styling Services,
Gurugram and Delhi NCR

After styling, a space feels resolved. Every piece serves the room and the room as a whole. Studio Loom selects furniture, textiles, and objects in proportion to the room, working from what the space needs, how it reads in its specific light, and how each material relationship holds together.

The service is suited to homes that are newly constructed or ready for a considered refresh.

What's Involved

The process begins with understanding the room as it currently exists and what it needs to feel complete. Studio Loom selects what the space calls for and places each piece in service of the whole. Every element earns its place.

What's Included
  • Space assessment and brief
  • Furniture curation and sourcing
  • Textile and soft furnishing selection
  • Object and art selection
  • Material editing across existing elements
  • Styling and installation
When Styling Is the Right Service
  • A space has been recently completed and needs a finishing layer
  • An existing space functions well but lacks visual coherence
  • A property is being prepared for photography, letting, or sale
