Studio Loom Consult
Gurugram, Delhi NCR & Pan India

A focused first visit to your space, before you commit to a project. We walk through it with you, understand how it will be used, and leave you with clear direction on layout, budget and next steps.

Chat on WhatsApp Send a brief

Who it's for

Anyone about to start on a space: a new home just handed over, a home you plan to renovate, an office or clinic you're leasing or fitting out, or a café, restaurant or boutique stay in the making.

What happens on site

We walk through every space with you and understand how it will be lived in or worked in. We review what shapes the interiors (services, ceilings, openings, light and layout), note what works, what doesn't, and what is cheaper to resolve now than later, and talk through budget and timeline, honestly.

What you receive

A short summary of observations and priorities, your plan marked up with layout direction, and a budget range and a realistic timeline, along with a recommended next step if you'd like to continue with us.

Common Questions

Frequently asked.

Is the Consult the same as your Design Consultation service?

No. The Consult is a short first visit that gives you direction. Design Consultation is our full design-only service, with complete drawings and specifications for your contractor.

What is not included in the Consult?

The Consult is a design-level review. It is not a structural, legal or engineering inspection, and it does not include construction drawings; those are part of our Design Consultation and Turnkey services.

How much does the Consult cost?

Every space is different, so the fee depends on its size and type. Message us on WhatsApp with a few details and we'll share it before you book. If you go ahead with the project, the fee is credited to it.

Do I have to hire Studio Loom after the Consult?

No. You keep everything we give you. If you do continue with us, the fee is credited toward your project.

Can you do the Consult before I get possession?

Yes. We can begin with your floor plan and visit once you have access to the space.
