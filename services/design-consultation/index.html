Common Questions

Frequently asked.

What is included in an interior design consultation?

Studio Loom's design consultation produces a complete design package: spatial plans, material and finish specifications, furniture schedules, lighting design, and detailed drawings. You or your contractor manage execution using the documentation.

At what stage of a project can I bring in an interior designer?

Studio Loom's design consultation service can be engaged at any stage of a project, from a blank space to one that is partially built or mid-renovation.

What is the difference between design consultation and turnkey?

Design consultation provides the complete design documentation for your contractor to execute. Turnkey means Studio Loom manages execution directly. Both services begin with the same design process.

Can I use my own contractor with Studio Loom's design?

Yes. Design consultation is specifically structured for clients who have an existing contractor or prefer to manage execution independently. Studio Loom provides the complete documentation; your contractor executes from it.
