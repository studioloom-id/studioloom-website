Our Approach to Residential Design

Every Studio Loom residential project begins with spatial planning. We resolve how the home works before we resolve how it looks. Layout is decided first. Materials are chosen for how they age and how they hold their quality over years of use. Furniture is specified for proportion and longevity.

The result is a home that functions precisely and feels particular to you, to your way of living, and to the material quality of contemporary Indian making at its best. Studio Loom is based in Gurugram (Gurgaon) and designs across Delhi NCR: Delhi, Noida, Greater Noida, Faridabad, and surrounding areas.

Common Questions

Frequently asked.

Does Studio Loom design apartments in DLF, Emaar, and other Gurugram developments?

Yes. Studio Loom regularly works on residential interiors in DLF, Emaar, M3M, Godrej, and Sobha developments across Gurugram. Contact us to confirm if your development is not listed.

What is the difference between luxury and premium interior design?

At Studio Loom, both terms refer to design quality and material specification rather than a fixed price bracket. The finish level (premium, luxury, or ultra-luxury) is defined by material category, degree of customisation, and specification depth, confirmed at brief stage.

How involved do I need to be during my home's interior design project?

Your involvement is structured around key decisions: brief, concept direction, material sign-off. Studio Loom manages coordination and progress between milestones. You are not expected to coordinate between teams or chase approvals.

What is the minimum scope for a residential interior design project?

Studio Loom works on full-home projects and specific-room projects above a defined minimum. Contact us to discuss your brief and confirm fit.
