Luxury Interior Design
for Homes in Gurugram

Your home in Gurugram should work as well as it looks. Studio Loom designs residential interiors for apartments and homes in DLF, Emaar, M3M, Godrej, and Sobha developments, built around how you actually live and how you use each room.

A well-designed home is a system that holds daily life with precision: the right spatial plan, materials chosen for how they age in Indian light, furniture specified for how it lives alongside the room's architecture. Studio Loom also works across Delhi NCR, including Delhi, Noida, and Faridabad.

Who We Design For

Homes that carry the soul of their owners.

Our clients are professionals and families who are specific about how they want their home to function and to feel. They are looking for a studio that will design their apartment from first principles, from the specifics of how they live. Premium apartment interior design in Gurugram is our primary practice.

Our Approach to Residential Design

Every Studio Loom residential project begins with spatial planning. We resolve how the home works before we resolve how it looks. Layout is decided first. Materials are chosen for how they age and how they hold their quality over years of use. Furniture is specified for proportion and longevity.

The result is a home that functions precisely and feels particular — to you, to your way of living, and to the material quality of contemporary Indian making at its best.

Studio Loom works across three service types for residential projects in Gurugram and Delhi NCR:

  • Turnkey Interior Design — Studio Loom manages design, procurement, execution, and handover. You receive a finished home.
  • Design Consultation — Studio Loom produces the complete design package. Your contractor manages execution.
  • Interior Styling — Considered curation for spaces that are newly completed or ready for a refresh.

Common Questions

Frequently asked.

Does Studio Loom design apartments in DLF, Emaar, and other Gurugram developments?

Yes. Studio Loom regularly works on residential interiors in DLF, Emaar, M3M, Godrej, and Sobha developments across Gurugram. Contact us to confirm if your development is not listed.

What is the difference between luxury and premium interior design?

At Studio Loom, both terms refer to design quality and material specification rather than a fixed price bracket. The finish level — premium, luxury, or ultra-luxury — is defined by material category, degree of customisation, and specification depth, confirmed at brief stage.

How involved do I need to be during my home's interior design project?

Your involvement is structured around key decisions — brief, concept direction, material sign-off. Studio Loom manages coordination and progress between milestones. You are not expected to coordinate between teams or chase approvals.

What is the minimum scope for a residential interior design project?

Studio Loom works on full-home projects and specific-room projects above a defined minimum. Contact us to discuss your brief and confirm fit.
