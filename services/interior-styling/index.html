Common Questions

Frequently asked.

What is interior styling?

Interior styling is the considered completion of a space: the selection and placement of furniture, textiles, and objects in proportion to the room. Studio Loom works from how the space reads as a whole, bringing coherence to newly built or existing interiors.

Is interior styling the same as interior design?

Interior styling is a distinct service. Interior design involves spatial planning, material specification, and architectural decisions for the space itself. Styling is the finishing layer of furniture curation, textiles, objects, and placement, applied to a space that is already built or near completion.

Can Studio Loom style a space I have already furnished?

Yes. Studio Loom works with existing furniture and objects as well as new curation. The process assesses what the space needs, what stays, what moves, and what is added.

How long does interior styling take?

A styling engagement typically takes one to three days on site, depending on the scale of the space. It begins with a briefing session to understand the room and what it needs to feel resolved.
