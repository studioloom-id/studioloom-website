Hospitality Interior Design,
India

Guests experience a hospitality space before they experience the product. How they enter, where light falls, how materials read at that hour — these things register before the menu is opened.

Studio Loom designs with that sequence in mind.

Our Approach to Hospitality

Every sequence,
considered.

Hospitality interiors are designed around sequence and experience. Studio Loom considers how a guest moves through the space, where the eye rests, how materials read under task and ambient lighting, and how the environment holds its character across a full service. The same spatial discipline that defines our residential work applies here.

Common Questions

Frequently asked.

Does Studio Loom design restaurant interiors?

Yes. Studio Loom designs restaurants, bars, cafes, and boutique hotel spaces. Hospitality interior design is a core practice at the studio.

What does turnkey interior design mean for a restaurant or hospitality project?

Turnkey for a hospitality project means Studio Loom manages the entire scope from design concept to opening-ready handover — including design, procurement, contractor coordination, and final styling.

Does Studio Loom work on boutique hotel interiors?

Yes. Studio Loom designs boutique hotels, guesthouses, and hospitality environments alongside restaurants, bars, and cafes.
