Common Questions

Frequently asked.

What does turnkey interior design include?

Turnkey interior design at Studio Loom includes spatial planning, design development, material specification, custom furniture coordination, procurement, site supervision, styling, and final handover. The entire project is managed under one scope, with a single point of contact throughout.

What is the difference between turnkey and design consultation?

Design consultation means Studio Loom produces the complete design package — plans, specifications, and documentation — and you or your contractor manage execution. Turnkey means Studio Loom manages execution directly, under the same scope as the design.

How involved do I need to be during a turnkey project?

Your involvement is focused on decisions that shape the project — brief, concept direction, material sign-off — at clear milestones. Studio Loom manages coordination, contractor oversight, and site progress between those milestones.

Does Studio Loom stay involved during construction?

Yes. Studio Loom oversees execution, reviews work in progress, and works directly with the contractor throughout construction. The space you receive matches the space that was designed.

Does Studio Loom offer turnkey interior design for commercial and hospitality projects?

Yes. Studio Loom delivers turnkey interior design across residential, commercial, and hospitality projects in Gurugram and across Delhi NCR.
