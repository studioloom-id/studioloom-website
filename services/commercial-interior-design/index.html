Common Questions

Frequently asked.

Does Studio Loom design office interiors in Gurugram?

Yes. Studio Loom designs office and workspace interiors in Gurugram and across Delhi NCR, across all three service types: turnkey, design consultation, and interior styling.

What types of commercial spaces does Studio Loom design?

Studio Loom works on office and corporate workspace interiors, co-working environments, and retail spaces across Gurugram and Delhi NCR.

How is commercial interior design different from residential at Studio Loom?

The design process follows the same discipline: spatial planning first, materials chosen for durability and character. The brief, performance requirements, and project timeline differ between commercial and residential projects. Studio Loom applies the same rigour to both.
